returns of the defined factor spreads, expressed as decimals, unannualized, and
without benchmark adjustment. For other snapshots, use the return basis specified
in their metric definitions. Under the bundled runtime configuration, forecast the

not forecasts; do not copy, annualize, or convert them unless the selected

configured return quantity over the configured forward horizon, given the supplied
evidence—not its median or most likely single scenario. This is a point estimate,
not a calibrated distribution; do not invent or report scenario probabilities.

excess-return basis; any required conversion is outside this forecast.
